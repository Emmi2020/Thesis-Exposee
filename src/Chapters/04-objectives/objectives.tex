\section{Objectives}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item Analyze the existing D-FMEA database (H1B and H1P product families) to understand structure and patterns.
    
    \item Characterize differences between pump/motor-level, functional group-level, and component-level D-FMEAs.
    
    \item Define an AI support model that identifies what can be automatically suggested versus what requires human judgment.
    
    \item Demonstrate feasibility through a prototype applied to a representative functional group.
    
    \item Provide recommendations for integrating AI-assisted D-FMEA creation into the development process.
\end{enumerate}
