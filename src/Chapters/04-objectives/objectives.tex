\section{Objectives}

\subsection*{Primary Objectives}

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Analyze} the existing D-FMEA database in the Hydrostatics department, focusing on H1B and H1P product families, to understand structure, patterns, and decision logic.
    
    \item \textbf{Characterize} the differences between pump/motor-level, functional group-level, and component-level D-FMEAs to establish clear boundaries for AI assistance.
    
    \item \textbf{Define} an AI support model that identifies what can be automatically reused or suggested from the existing D-FMEA pool versus what requires explicit human engineering judgment.
    
    \item \textbf{Demonstrate} the feasibility of AI-guided D-FMEA creation through a concrete, representative example of a new functional group.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Secondary Objectives}

\begin{itemize}
    \item Provide recommendations for implementing an AI-assisted D-FMEA tool in the development process.
    \item Identify knowledge management best practices for maintaining and extending the D-FMEA pool.
    \item Assess efficiency gains and risk reduction potential from AI-guided automation.
\end{itemize}
