\section{Objectives}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item Analyzing the existing D-FMEA Excel files for H1B and H1P product families to understand their structure, content, and patterns.\footnote{Files will be ingested and profiled within Databricks, explicitly targeting the historical data patterns of the H1B and H1P product families.}
    
    \item Understanding differences between pump/motor-level, functional group-level, and component-level D-FMEAs. 
    
    \item Defining an AI support model that identifies what can be automatically suggested versus what requires human judgment.\footnote{This involves identifying which standard rows can be safely autopopulated versus where novel design changes require human engineering validation.}
    
    \item Demonstrating feasibility through a prototype applied to a representative functional group.\footnote{Engineering an end-to-end data pipeline that takes a new layout as input, runs a vector search, and automatically generates a pre-populated D-FMEA template.}
    
    \item Providing recommendations for integrating AI-assisted D-FMEA creation into the development process.
\end{enumerate}
