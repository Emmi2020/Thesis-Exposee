\section{Problem Description}

\subsection*{Current Situation}

Hydrostatic pump and motor design at Danfoss follows repetitive patterns. Existing D-FMEAs for similar components (H1B and H1P product families) are stored in centralized databases as Excel files, containing valuable insights about failure modes, severity assessments, and mitigation actions.

\subsection*{Problem}

When developing new functional groups or components, engineers typically reference only the most similar existing D-FMEA through copy-paste, rather than systematically analyzing the entire available D-FMEA pool. This approach leads to:

\begin{itemize}
    \item Overlooked critical design issues present in other historical D-FMEAs
    \item Incomplete risk assessments
    \item Inefficient manual processes
    \item Inconsistent decision-making across similar components
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Why This Matters}

Inadequate D-FMEAs can result in design flaws reaching production, compromising product reliability and customer satisfaction. Systematic AI support could transform this process by intelligently identifying, categorizing, and recommending relevant actions from the entire knowledge pool.
