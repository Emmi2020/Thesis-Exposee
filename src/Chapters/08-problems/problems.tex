\section{Potential Challenges}

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Data Access and Privacy:} The D-FMEA database contains proprietary design information. Solution: Work within Danfoss data governance policies; anonymize sensitive data for documentation; sign appropriate confidentiality agreements.
    
    \item \textbf{Domain Complexity:} Understanding hydrostatic design and FMEA domain knowledge requires specialized expertise. Solution: Engage experienced FMEA practitioners; conduct structured interviews; document domain terminology and concepts early.
    
    \item \textbf{AI Model Accuracy:} Semantic matching and classification of failure modes may not be perfect. Solution: Use hybrid approach combining AI suggestions with expert verification; define clear confidence thresholds; validate against historical decisions.
    
    \item \textbf{Generalization:} The case study example may not represent all scenarios. Solution: Select a representative functional group; discuss limitations and boundary conditions in thesis.
    
    \item \textbf{Proof-of-Concept Implementation:} Developing a functional AI tool may be ambitious. Solution: Start with a prototype or simulation; focus on demonstrating feasibility rather than production-ready software.
    
    \item \textbf{Balancing Automation and Responsibility:} Over-automation risks overlooking critical decisions. Solution: Clearly define AI support levels; ensure human engineers remain in the loop for risk decisions; design for explainability.
\end{itemize}
