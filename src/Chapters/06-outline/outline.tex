\section{Thesis Structure}

The thesis will follow this structure:

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Introduction}: Context, problem statement, and research objectives
    \item \textbf{Background}: FMEA methodology, AI in engineering, knowledge management
    \item \textbf{Current State Analysis}: HY D-FMEA database structure and creation process
    \item \textbf{AI Support Model}: What can be automated versus what requires human judgment
    \item \textbf{Prototype and Case Study}: Proof-of-concept implementation for one functional group
    \item \textbf{Discussion}: Feasibility, limitations, and integration potential
    \item \textbf{Conclusion}: Recommendations and future work
\end{enumerate}
