\section{Research Questions}

\subsection*{Main Research Question}

How can AI support the creation of Design-FMEAs for new hydrostatic functional groups by intelligently leveraging the existing D-FMEA knowledge pool, while maintaining the rigor required for design-to-production processes?

\subsection*{Sub-questions}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item What structural and semantic differences exist between pump/motor, functional group, and component D-FMEAs in the HY department?
    
    \item What elements within existing D-FMEAs (for H1B and H1P products) can be automatically transferred or reused in new designs, and which elements require domain-expert decision-making?
    
    \item How can an AI system intelligently classify and match failure modes, severity assessments, and mitigation actions from the historical D-FMEA pool to new functional groups?
    
    \item What is an appropriate support model (retrieval, classification, recommendation) that balances automation with engineering responsibility?
    
    \item Can we demonstrate feasibility through a concrete example of AI-guided D-FMEA creation for a new functional group?
\end{enumerate}
