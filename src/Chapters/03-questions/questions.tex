\section{Research Questions}

\subsection*{Main Research Question}

How can AI support the creation of Design-FMEAs for new Hydrostatic functional groups by leveraging the existing D-FMEA knowledge pool, while maintaining the rigor required for design-to-production processes?

\subsection*{Sub-questions}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item What elements within existing D-FMEAs can be automatically suggested or reused, and which require human engineering judgment?
    
    \item Which AI approach (semantic matching, similarity ranking) is most effective for suggesting failure modes and mitigation actions from the historical D-FMEA pool?
    
    \item Can we demonstrate feasibility and value through a working prototype for one representative functional group?
    
\end{enumerate}
