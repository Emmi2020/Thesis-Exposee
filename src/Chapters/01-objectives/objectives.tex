\section{Internship Assignment}

The Dual Study program is a structured educational pathway that combines academic studies with practical work experience at the partner company. The practical phase allows the student to rotate through different departments and allows the student to work on different projects whether in a team or independently. As a result, the student obtains a comprehensive understanding of the company's operations, culture, and the practical application of theoretical knowledge in real-world projects.

As an integral part of the Study program, the internship was conducted in various teams and departments within the company, providing a wide range of experiences and exposure to different aspects of the company's operations. The teams included the Global Supply Chain management team, The Teststand Technologies team in Neumünster and in Ames, United States.
The majority of the time was spent working on various tasks, mainly related to optimizing business processes through \ac{IT} and software development. 
Most of the tasks were assigned by the Teststand Technologies team. The team is composed of Hydaraulic Engineers, Software Developers, Automation Engineers and Maintenance Technicians to ensure the smooth operation of the Teststand in the production environment. 



\section{Company Expectations}

The company expects the intern to contribute to day-to-day work, support team processes, and apply academic knowledge in a professional environment.
Once the intern has familiarized themselves with the company's processes and workflows, more responsibilities and tasks get assigned, allowing them to take an active role in Projects.
