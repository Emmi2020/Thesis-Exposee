\section{Methods}

\subsection*{Research Approach}

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Literature Review:} Examine FMEA methodology, AI-assisted engineering, knowledge management in product development, and case studies of AI in quality assurance.
    
    \item \textbf{Data Analysis:} Conduct exploratory analysis of the existing D-FMEA database to understand structure, content patterns, and semantic relationships between failure modes and mitigation actions.
    
    \item \textbf{Expert Interviews:} Consult with FMEA practitioners and design engineers at Danfoss to validate findings and understand decision-making processes.
    
    \item \textbf{Use Case Modeling:} Develop a detailed case study demonstrating AI-guided D-FMEA creation for a new functional group (e.g., a new pump/motor variant with similar requirements).
    
    \item \textbf{Proof-of-Concept Development:} Implement or prototype an AI-assisted tool (using text classification, semantic matching, or knowledge graph techniques) to showcase feasibility.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Data Collection}

\begin{itemize}
    \item Access to Danfoss HY department's D-FMEA database (Excel files for H1B and H1P products)
    \item Technical documentation of existing designs and functional groups
    \item Interviews with domain experts and process owners
    \item Relevant academic and industry literature
\end{itemize}
