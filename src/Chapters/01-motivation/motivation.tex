\section{Motivation}

Design Failure Mode and Effects Analysis (D-FMEA) is essential for managing risk and quality in hydrostatic component design at Danfoss Power Solutions. However, engineers rely on copy-paste workflows when creating new D-FMEAs, using only one reference document instead of leveraging the full historical knowledge pool. This results in inefficient processes and risks overlooking critical design issues.

Artificial Intelligence has demonstrated potential to enhance decision-making and reduce errors in engineering processes. This thesis explores how AI can intelligently guide D-FMEA creation by automatically suggesting failure modes and mitigation actions from the existing knowledge pool. This demonstrates how Media Engineering skills can optimize engineering processes through advanced AI technologies.
