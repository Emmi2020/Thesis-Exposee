\section{Motivation}

In Industrial manufacturing, managing risk and ensuring quality are critical aspects of the design and production process. Design Failure Mode and Effects Analysis (D-FMEA)\footnote{D-FMEA: Conducted during the design phase (as opposed to Process FMEA for manufacturing)} is a widely used methodology to systematically identify potential failure modes, assess their impact, and implement mitigation actions. Within Danfoss Power Solutions's Hydrostatic division, D-FMEA is strategically deployed early in the design phase to proactively identify, evaluate, and control potential failure modes before the products are ever in production. However traditional D-FMEA processes are heavily document-centric, labor intensive, and often rely on historical knowledge stored in disparate Excel files. As Hydrostatic files grow in size and complexity, engineering face severe risk of overlooking critical design information. 

As a Media Engineering student, this thesis represents an ideela opportuniy to solve a complex data access and Human-computer interaction challenge. By implementing an automated pipeline to extract, index , match and recommend data from the pool directly increases the accuracy and reduces the time required for engineers to perform D-FMEA.
