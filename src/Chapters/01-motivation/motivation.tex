\section{Motivation}

Design Failure Mode and Effects Analysis (D-FMEA) is a critical quality assurance tool in the development and change management processes at Danfoss Power Solutions. The repetitive nature of hydrostatic component design leads to extensive copy-paste practices when creating new D-FMEAs, resulting in inefficient processes and potential oversight of critical design issues.

The application of AI-guided automation to D-FMEA creation offers significant potential to:

\begin{itemize}
    \item Improve efficiency by leveraging the existing D-FMEA knowledge pool within the Hydrostatics (HY) department
    \item Reduce human error through intelligent classification and reuse of proven solutions
    \item Ensure comprehensive coverage of design risks by systematically referencing all relevant historical D-FMEAs, not just the most similar one
    \item Support engineers in navigating the complex design-to-production process with AI-guided recommendations
\end{itemize}

This thesis combines quality engineering, knowledge management, and artificial intelligence to address a real business challenge in product development.
