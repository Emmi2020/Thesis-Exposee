\section{Company Overview}
The practical part of the Dual study program has been carried out at Danfoss as a host company, and it is introduced in this section.

Danfoss A/S is a multinational engineering company headquartered in Nordborg, Denmark. It operates in various sectors, including heating, cooling, drives, and mobile hydraulics. The company employs over 39,345 employees worldwide in more than 20 countries (as of 2025) \cite{DanfosAnnualReport2025}.
Danfoss products are sold in about 100 countries, generating approximately 10 billion euros in revenue.

The company operates globally in three segments: \ac{DCS}Danfoss Climate Solutions, \ac{DPD}Danfoss Power Electronics and Drives, and \ac{DPS}Danfoss Power Solutions. Within these segments, \ac{DPS} is the largest, employing over 16,000 people across 52 locations and generating revenue of approximately 4.1 billion euros (as of 2025), with 44\% of sales coming from other segments \cite{DanfossAnnualReport2025}.

\ac{DPS} design and manufactures hydraulic, electric, and electronic systems for mobile and industrial machinery. The segment's businesses are organized into several divisions being the Hydrostatics,Industrial,Fluid Conveyance, Editron and Controls and each focusing on different product lines and market needs.
