\section{Company Overview}
Danfoss is a multinational engineering company headquartered in Nordborg, Denmark. It operates in various sectors, including heating, cooling, drives, and mobile hydraulics. The company employs over 39,345 employees worldwide in more than 20 countries (as of 2025) \cite{DanfossAnnualReport2025}.
Danfoss products are sold in about 100 countries, generating approximately 10 billion euros in revenue.

The company operates globally in three segments: Danfoss Climate Solutions, Danfoss Power Electronics and Drives, and Danfoss Power Solutions. Within these segments, Danfoss Power Solutions is the largest, employing over 16,000 people across 52 locations and generating revenue of approximately 4.1 billion euros (as of 2025), with 44\% of sales coming from other segments \cite{DanfossAnnualReport2025}.
